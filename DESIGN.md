# MediLink Elite Design System

## Core Aesthetics
- **Primary Color**: Professional Navy Blue (`#1e3a8a`)
- **Danger/Accent Color**: Maroon (`#7f1d1d`)
- **Background**: Pure White (`#ffffff`)
- **Surface**: Very light slate/gray for cards (`#f8fafc`)
- **Typography**: Inter font, clean and readable under stress
- **Corner Rounding**: Moderate (12px to 16px) for a friendly yet clinical feel.
- **UI Feel**: Trustworthy, calm under pressure, medical-grade, fast, and accessible.

## Components
- **Buttons**: Navy for primary actions, Maroon for emergency actions.
- **Cards**: Light surface with subtle shadows and Navy or Maroon borders when highlighted.
